Privacy

Last updated 19 September 2026

Short version: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Nearly everything stays in your browser. The only personal data our server holds is a push subscription, and only if you turn notifications on.

Who runs this

Octopus Tracker is an independent hobby project by Alex McGuiness. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Octopus Energy Ltd. Questions, or a request to remove data, go to the project's GitHub issues (please don't post personal details there — just say what you need and you'll be told how to proceed).

What stays in your browser

These are kept in your browser's local storage and are never sent to our server:

  • Settings: region, tariff, gas tariff, whether gas is shown, forecast length, light/dark theme and notification choices.
  • Octopus API key and account number, if you choose to enter them for tariff detection or usage. They are sent only to Octopus's own API (api.octopus.energy), directly from your browser. Remove them any time with Settings → Clear stored credentials.
  • Cached data: recent prices and, if you've connected your account, a summary of your spend. It's kept for up to 48 hours so the app opens quickly and works offline.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

What our server stores

Only if you turn on push notifications. Then we store your browser's push address (a URL issued by your browser's push service) and its encryption keys, your region, your alert choices and when you subscribed. It's kept in a hosted database (Upstash Redis) and used only to send the alerts you picked. It is not linked to your name, email or Octopus account.

  • Turning notifications off in Settings deletes it. It's also removed if your push service reports the address is no longer valid.
  • Short-lived markers (up to 36 hours) stop the same alert being sent twice. They contain no personal details.
  • We never store your Octopus API key or account number on our server.

Other services involved

  • Octopus Energy: your browser fetches prices directly from their API, so they see your IP address, and your API key and account if you've connected them. Their privacy policy applies.
  • Vercel hosts this site and keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and requested pages), under their policy.
  • Push delivery goes through the push service run by your browser's maker (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple).
  • AgilePredict and Nord Pool supply forecast and wholesale data. Those requests are relayed through this site's hosting, so those services don't receive your details.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

Cookies

None. The local storage described above is used only for things you asked the app to do, such as remembering your region.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold about you, have it deleted, and withdraw consent at any time (turn notifications off). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Notification data is processed on the basis of your consent.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top will change with it. The source code is public on GitHub.
