Privacy

Last updated 19 September 2026

There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or ads. Almost everything stays in your browser. The one thing my server keeps is a push subscription, and only if you turn notifications on.

Who I am

I'm Alex McGuiness and I built this as a hobby. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Octopus Energy. If you have a question or want something removed, open an issue on GitHub. Please keep personal details out of it. Just say what you need and I'll tell you how to sort it.

What stays in your browser

This is saved in your browser's local storage and never sent to my server:

  • Your settings: region, tariff, gas tariff, whether gas is shown, forecast length, theme and notification choices.
  • Your Octopus API key and account number, if you enter them for tariff detection or usage. They go straight from your browser to Octopus (api.octopus.energy) and nowhere else. There's a button in Settings to clear them.
  • Recent prices, plus a summary of your spend if you've connected your account. These are kept for up to 48 hours so the app loads fast and works offline.

Clear this site's data in your browser and all of it goes.

What my server keeps

Only if you turn on push notifications. Then it stores your browser's push address (a URL from your browser's push service), its keys, your region, your alert choices and when you signed up. It sits in a hosted database (Upstash Redis) and is only used to send the alerts you picked. It isn't tied to your name, email or Octopus account.

  • Turn notifications off and it's deleted. It's also dropped if your push service says the address is dead.
  • To avoid sending the same alert twice, I keep short-lived markers for up to 36 hours. There's nothing personal in them.
  • Your API key and account number never reach my server.

Who else sees what

  • Octopus: your browser gets prices straight from their API, so they see your IP address, plus your API key and account if you've connected them. Their own privacy policy covers that.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps normal server logs, like IP addresses and the pages requested.
  • Push messages travel through whoever made your browser (Google, Mozilla or Apple).
  • Forecast and wholesale data come from AgilePredict and Nord Pool. Those requests are relayed through the site's host, so they don't get your details.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

Cookies

None. The local storage above is only used for things you asked for, like remembering your region.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what I hold about you, ask for it to be deleted, and withdraw your consent by turning notifications off. Notification data is handled on the basis of your consent. If you're not happy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes
